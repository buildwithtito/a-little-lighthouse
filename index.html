THE TESTIMONY

From Shadow To Light

Growing up, I was bullied for my dark skin and faced deep isolation. I used to pray at night that God would change my shade—thinking a different skin tone could fix my little life.

"I didn't know then that my darkness was where His light would shine brightest."

Through crisis centers, bipolar 2 diagnoses, medications, and low-lows, doctors offered therapy. But God added all-nighters, His Word, and an anchor that never moves.

"Only Jesus met me in the middle of the noise and gave me a brand new song."

He turned "Miss Melanin" into a beacon for the lost, the skeptical, the broken, and the searching. We don't need a formal pulpit to preach the Gospel—just an open heart.

"Pointing every vessel back to the only One who saves."
